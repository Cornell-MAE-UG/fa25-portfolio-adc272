For Cornell's Engineering Mechanical Design Class (ENGRD 2210), we had the clients of Cornell CALS Extension, E&J Gallo Winery, and National Grape. The problem they came to us with was the spotted lantern fly, an invasive species, which have destroyed hundreds of grape harvests. If we can limit their destruction, we can save farmers thousands of dollars and reduce food waste.

Problem Statement:
Spotted lantern flies gather on grape vines, but they also gather on other plants that we don’t harvest, like the Tree of Heaven. How can we leverage this unique quality and lure spotted lantern flies to specific locations away from the vineyards to be exterminated?

Proposed Direction:
Concept Solution 1) Decoy Tree-like Lure
Humidifier is a part of this structure or attached to a Tree of Heaven
The humidifier will spray attractive fumes and poisonous fumes/insecticides at a to be determined rate
- Grape Berry Moth Spray
Another possibility is to incorporate the poison as an artificial sap for the humidifier
The diffused scent could attract the SLFs and bait them to drink the sap and be poisoned
End-of-semester proof-of-concept: We can build the fumigator attachment that will be added to tall poles, and we can design a linkage that can attach to a standard pole or tree.
Concept Solution 2) High Frequency Hotspot Fence
Wrap wires around a fence with decoy grape vines etc. that’s not too close in vicinity to the grapes
Attach wires to a speaker that plays a signal at exactly 60 Hz 
Minimum Viable Product
Our minimum viable product is a humidifier with a linkage design made to attach to any standardized telephone pole.
